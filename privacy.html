Privacy

Short version: we collect what we need to run the site and fulfill orders. We do not sell your data.

Last updated 24 September 2026

Horseshoe Round Me operates horseshoeroundme.com. This page explains what happens to information you share with us.

What we collect

  • Newsletter and contact forms — email, optional callsign/name, and message content. Forms are processed by Netlify Forms.
  • Basic site logs — standard hosting telemetry Netlify may keep (IP, user agent, request path) to keep the lights on and fight abuse.
  • Checkout partners — if you buy apparel/gear, Printify (and its payment processors) handle payment and shipping details. Digitals: Payhip. Stickers: Stripe. Those companies have their own privacy policies.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not invent mailing lists from thin air.
  • We are not a crisis provider; do not send emergency information to the contact form.

Cookies and embeds

The marketing site is mostly static. Third-party destinations (Printify, Payhip, Stripe, social platforms, YouTube) may set their own cookies when you leave our pages.

Contact

Questions about this policy: use the contact form. For partner-held order data, contact Printify, Payhip, or Stripe directly.
