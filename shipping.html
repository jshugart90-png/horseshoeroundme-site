Shipping and returns

Print-on-demand is not next-day doorstep magic. Digitals are. Stickers are their own lane.

Last updated 24 September 2026

Apparel and gear (Printify)

Items sold through horseshoe-round-me.printify.me are print-on-demand. Typical production and shipping window is about 7–12 business days after you order, sometimes longer during peak seasons. Tracking and carrier details come from Printify / the print provider once the order ships.

We do not claim APO/FPO/DPO eligibility here unless Printify checkout clearly offers it for your address. Check the store at checkout.

Returns (Printify)

Printify's Pop-Up Store offers a 30-day returns window for eligible order issues (defects, wrong item, and related cases per their policy). Start with the order confirmation / Printify support tools. See also Printify's terms on the store.

Digitals (Payhip)

Roast Pack and Lock Screen Drop deliver instantly after Payhip checkout. No physical shipping. Refunds follow Payhip's rules for digital goods.

Stickers (Stripe)

Sticker packs are sold via a Stripe payment link. Shipping details appear in the purchase flow / receipt. Contact via the Stripe receipt if something goes sideways.

Questions

Order problems: use the partner that charged you first. General site questions: contact form. Crisis: Resources / 988 then 1.
