# U.S. Veterans & Military Resources Directory

Curated for **Horseshoe Round Me** ([horseshoeroundme.com](https://horseshoeroundme.com)).

Direct, practical links for Veterans, service members, Guard/Reserve, caregivers, and military families. Prefer official .gov sources and established nonprofits. Always confirm eligibility on the organization’s site.

**Crisis:** If you or someone you know is in crisis, dial **988** then press **1**, text **838255**, or visit [VeteransCrisisLine.net](https://www.veteranscrisisline.net/).

---

## Crisis & Immediate Help

### [Veterans Crisis Line](https://www.veteranscrisisline.net/)

24/7 confidential support for Veterans, service members, Guard/Reserve, and their families. Dial 988 then press 1, text 838255, or chat online. No VA enrollment required.

### [National Call Center for Homeless Veterans](https://www.va.gov/homeless/nationalcallcenter.asp)

Free, confidential 24/7 VA hotline (877-424-3838) for Veterans who are homeless or at risk of losing housing. Counselors connect you to local VA homeless services.

### [Military OneSource](https://www.militaryonesource.mil/)

DoD-funded 24/7 information and referral hub for service members, families, and survivors—counseling, financial help, relocation, and crisis support.

### [Give an Hour](https://giveanhour.org/)

Nonprofit network that connects military members, Veterans, and families to free or low-cost mental health care and peer support.

### [The Headstrong Project](https://theheadstrongproject.org/)

Confidential, no-cost mental health treatment for post-9/11 Veterans and families; focuses on trauma-informed care without VA bureaucracy.

### [VA Suicide Prevention](https://www.va.gov/health-care/health-needs-conditions/mental-health/suicide-prevention/)

VA suicide-prevention overview with crisis contacts, local resource locator links, and how to reach suicide prevention coordinators.

## Official VA & DoD

### [U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs (VA.gov)](https://www.va.gov/)

Primary portal for VA health care, disability claims, education benefits, home loans, life insurance, and burial benefits. Apply and manage benefits online.

### [milConnect](https://milconnect.dmdc.osd.mil/milconnect/)

DoD portal to manage DEERS, view military records, print proof of coverage, and access transition documents including VMET.

### [TRICARE](https://www.tricare.mil/)

Military health plan for active duty, retirees, Guard/Reserve, and eligible family members. Enrollment, plan comparison, and provider search.

### [Defense Enrollment Eligibility Reporting System (DEERS)](https://tricare.mil/DEERS)

Official system that records eligibility for military benefits. Update addresses, dependents, and ID card information through milConnect or a RAPIDS site.

### [VA Mental Health](https://www.mentalhealth.va.gov/)

VA overview of mental health programs, how to get care, and links to crisis resources and specialty clinics.

### [DoD Transition Assistance Program (TAP)](https://www.dodtap.mil/)

Official DoD TAP portal for separating and retiring service members—mandatory briefs, career readiness standards, and DD Form 2648.

### [DoD SkillBridge](https://skillbridge.mil/)

Allows eligible service members within 180 days of separation to train with civilian employers while still on active duty (with command approval).

### [My HealtheVet / VA Health & Benefits App](https://www.va.gov/)

Start from VA.gov to refill prescriptions, message care teams, and manage appointments and benefits on web or mobile.

## Benefits, Claims & Disability

### [Disabled American Veterans (DAV)](https://www.dav.org/)

VSO that files and appeals VA disability claims at no cost, plus transportation to VA medical appointments and employment programs.

### [Veterans of Foreign Wars (VFW)](https://www.vfw.org/)

National VSO offering free VA claims assistance, advocacy, financial grants, and local post support for combat and overseas Veterans.

### [The American Legion](https://www.legion.org/)

Largest U.S. veterans service organization; provides claims help, community programs, and advocacy on VA and DoD issues.

### [Paralyzed Veterans of America (PVA)](https://pva.org/)

VSO focused on Veterans with spinal cord injury/disease, MS, and ALS—claims representation, advocacy, and specialized care support.

### [AMVETS](https://www.amvets.org/)

Veterans service organization providing free claims representation, employment services, and local posts open to nearly all honorably discharged Veterans.

### [Iraq and Afghanistan Veterans of America (IAVA)](https://iava.org/)

Post-9/11 Veterans advocacy organization focused on mental health, benefits access, and policy for GWOT-era service members.

### [National Veterans Legal Services Program (NVLSP)](https://nvlsp.org/)

Nonprofit law firm that trains VSOs and provides free legal help on VA benefits, discharge upgrades, and caregiver appeals.

### [VA Disability Compensation](https://www.va.gov/disability/)

Official VA page to file and manage disability compensation claims for service-connected conditions.

### [VA Veteran Readiness and Employment (VR&E)](https://www.va.gov/careers-employment/vocational-rehabilitation/)

Chapter 31 program for Veterans with service-connected disabilities—training, education, job placement, and independent living support.

### [VA Claims Appeals (Board of Veterans’ Appeals)](https://www.va.gov/decision-reviews/)

Official paths to request a Higher-Level Review, Supplemental Claim, or Board Appeal if you disagree with a VA benefits decision.

### [Cohen Veterans Network](https://www.cohenveteransnetwork.org/)

National network of mental health clinics offering accessible care for post-9/11 Veterans, service members, and families.

### [Wounded Warrior Project](https://www.woundedwarriorproject.org/)

Programs for post-9/11 wounded, ill, or injured Veterans—mental health, peer support, benefits counseling, and family services at no cost.

### [Hope For The Warriors](https://www.hopeforthewarriors.org/)

Supports post-9/11 service members, Veterans, and families with clinical care, peer programs, and transition resources.

### [PsychArmor](https://psycharmor.org/)

Free online courses that train clinicians, employers, and communities to work effectively with Veterans and military families.

### [Team Red, White & Blue (Team RWB)](https://teamrwb.org/)

Community fitness and belonging organization connecting Veterans through workouts, events, and local chapters nationwide.

### [VA Mental Health — Get Help](https://www.mentalhealth.va.gov/get-help/index.asp)

VA landing page for getting mental health help, including crisis options and how to start care through VA.

## Housing & Homelessness

### [VA Homeless Programs](https://department.va.gov/homeless/)

Overview of VA housing and homelessness services, including how to reach local Homeless Coordinators and national hotlines.

### [HUD-VASH](https://department.va.gov/homeless/hud-vash/)

Joint HUD–VA program pairing Housing Choice Vouchers with VA case management for homeless Veterans and their families.

### [Supportive Services for Veteran Families (SSVF)](https://department.va.gov/homeless/what-is-permanent-housing/)

VA-funded community program that prevents homelessness and rapidly re-houses very low-income Veteran families.

### [National Coalition for Homeless Veterans (NCHV)](https://nchv.org/)

National advocacy and capacity-building group focused on ending Veteran homelessness; connects Veterans to local providers.

### [VA Home Loans](https://www.va.gov/housing-assistance/home-loans/)

VA-backed home loan benefits for eligible Veterans, service members, and surviving spouses—purchase, refinance, and Certificate of Eligibility.

### [Operation Homefront — Housing](https://operationhomefront.org/housing/)

Nonprofit housing programs for military and Veteran families, including transitional and permanent housing support.

### [Volunteers of America — Veteran Services](https://www.voa.org/services/supportive-services-for-veterans-and-their-families/)

National nonprofit providing housing, case management, employment, and behavioral health services for Veterans and families.

### [HUD Homeless Veterans / HUD-VASH Overview](https://www.hud.gov/helping-americans/housing-choice-vouchers-homeless-veterans/)

HUD’s public-facing overview of HUD-VASH vouchers and how public housing authorities partner with VA for Veteran housing.

Personalized employment navigation for transitioning service members, with referrals to partner career programs.

## Education & GI Bill

### [VA Education & Training (GI Bill)](https://www.va.gov/education/)

Official VA hub for Post-9/11 GI Bill, Montgomery GI Bill, and other education benefits—eligibility, applications, and payment info.

### [GI Bill Comparison Tool / Education Benefits Overview](https://www.va.gov/education/about-gi-bill-benefits/)

Plain-language VA guide to GI Bill benefit types, housing allowance, and what each chapter covers.

### [Student Veterans of America (SVA)](https://studentveterans.org/)

National network of 1,600+ campus chapters supporting student Veterans with leadership programs, advocacy, and peer community.

### [VA Yellow Ribbon Program](https://www.va.gov/education/about-gi-bill-benefits/post-9-11/yellow-ribbon-program/)

Partnership between VA and schools that can cover tuition beyond the Post-9/11 GI Bill annual cap at participating institutions.

### [Veterans Education Success (VES)](https://vetsedsuccess.org/)

Nonprofit that helps student Veterans with GI Bill problems, school complaints, career counseling, and free legal advice related to education benefits.

### [VA VET TEC 2.0 / High-Tech Training](https://www.va.gov/education/)

VA education portal covering high-tech training pathways and related education benefits for eligible Veterans and service members.

### [GI Bill Hotline](https://www.va.gov/education/)

Call 888-442-4551 (888-GIBILL-1) for official help with GI Bill applications, payments, and school certification questions.

### [Tragedy Assistance Program for Survivors (TAPS)](https://www.taps.org/)

24/7 grief support for survivors of fallen military members—peer mentoring, counseling referrals, and survivor community.

### [Fisher House Foundation](https://fisherhouse.org/)

Provides free lodging near military and VA medical centers for families of wounded, ill, or injured service members and Veterans.

### [Blue Star Families](https://bluestarfam.org/)

Nonprofit connecting military and Veteran families through local chapters, research, spouse career support, and community programs.

### [National Military Family Association (NMFA)](https://www.militaryfamily.org/)

Advocacy and programs for military families, including spouse scholarships and policy work on quality-of-life issues.

### [Operation Homefront](https://operationhomefront.org/)

Financial assistance, housing support, and family programs for active-duty and Veteran families facing hardship.

### [Soldiers’ Angels](https://soldiersangels.org/)

Volunteer-driven aid including care packages, food assistance, and support for deployed troops, Veterans, and military families.

### [Semper Fi & America’s Fund](https://thefund.org/)

Lifetime case management and financial support for critically wounded, ill, or injured service members, Veterans, and families across all branches.

### [Elizabeth Dole Foundation — Hidden Heroes](https://hiddenheroes.org/)

National community and resource hub for military and Veteran caregivers, including education, peer connection, and advocacy.

### [Code of Support Foundation](https://www.codeofsupport.org/)

Connects Veterans, service members, caregivers, and families to vetted resources through case coordination and the PATRIOTlink network.

### [Service Women’s Action Network (SWAN)](https://www.servicewomensactionnetwork.org/)

## Legal Aid & Justice

### [National Veterans Legal Services Program (NVLSP)](https://nvlsp.org/)

Free and low-cost legal representation on VA claims, discharge upgrades, and related Veterans benefits disputes.

### [NVLSP Lawyers Serving Warriors](https://nvlsp.org/what-we-do/lawyers-serving-warriors/)

NVLSP program matching Veterans with pro bono attorneys for complex benefits and discharge-upgrade cases.

### [VA Discharge Upgrade Information](https://www.va.gov/discharge-upgrade-eligibility/)

Official VA guidance on how character of discharge affects benefits and where to start a discharge review or upgrade.

### [Department of Labor USERRA / VETS](https://www.dol.gov/agencies/vets/programs/userra)

Federal enforcement and guidance on Uniformed Services Employment and Reemployment Rights Act job protections for service members.

### [SWAN Case Management & Legal Referrals](https://www.servicewomensactionnetwork.org/)

SWAN maintains vetted pro bono resources and a 24/7 assistance hotline (888-502-8755) for servicewomen and women Veterans facing legal and benefits barriers.

## Financial & Emergency Assistance

### [VFW Unmet Needs](https://www.vfw.org/assistance/financial-grants)

VFW grants (typically up to $2,500) for eligible military families facing financial hardship for basic living expenses.

### [American Legion Temporary Financial Assistance](https://www.legion.org/get-involved/community-programs/temporary-financial-assistance/about)

One-time grants through local Legion posts to help minor children of eligible Veterans and service members with essential needs.

### [Army Emergency Relief](https://www.armyemergencyrelief.org/)

Official Army aid society providing interest-free loans and grants to Soldiers and families in financial emergency.

### [Navy-Marine Corps Relief Society](https://www.nmcrs.org/)

Financial assistance, budgeting help, and emergency aid for Sailors, Marines, and their families.

### [Air & Space Forces Aid Society](https://afas.org/)

Official charity of the Air Force and Space Force offering emergency loans/grants and education assistance for Airmen, Guardians, and families.

### [Operation Homefront Critical Financial Assistance](https://operationhomefront.org/)

Emergency financial help for qualifying military and Veteran families covering essentials like rent, utilities, and food.

### [VA Pension](https://www.va.gov/pension/)

Needs-based monthly payments for wartime Veterans (and some survivors) with limited income who meet age or disability criteria.

### [Coast Guard Mutual Assistance](https://mycgma.org/)

Official Coast Guard aid society offering emergency financial assistance, education loans/grants, and counseling for Coast Guard members and families.
